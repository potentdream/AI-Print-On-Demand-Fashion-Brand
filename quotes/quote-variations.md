# Quote Variations

For each seed quote, 10 on-brand variations that keep the tone **bold, cheeky,
feminine, confident**. Use these to A/B designs, build collections, or spin off
new SKUs. Each variation is tagged with mood and best product fit.

> Generated/extended with `npm run quote-variations -- --quote "<seed>"`.
> Always eyeball new variations for brand voice + non-offensiveness before use.

## Variation writing rules

1. **Keep the rhythm.** Most brand quotes use a two- or three-beat contrast
   (`Sweet … Savage …`). Preserve the cadence.
2. **Keep the contrast.** Soft word vs. sharp word is the engine of the voice.
3. **Stay feminine + confident**, never mean-spirited or vulgar.
4. **Desi variations stay affectionate** — celebrate, don't caricature.
5. **Punchy > long.** Aim for ≤ 6 words per beat; great on a chest print.
6. **Check uniqueness** against `quotes-master.md` before promoting to a SKU.
