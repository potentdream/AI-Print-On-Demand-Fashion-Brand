  happens here. **Start by reading `aunty/AGENTS.md`**, which points to the
  living documents (`aunty/CLAUDE.md`, `aunty/PROGRESS.md`,
  `aunty/DECISIONS.md`, `aunty/SPEC.md`).
- **Root (`automation/`, `designs/`, `quotes/`, `scripts/`, `templates/`,
  root `package.json`) — dormant.** Print-on-demand fashion-brand workflow
  from an earlier project. Do not modify or delete unless the human asks.

Work happens on branch `claude/new-session-9b65cc`. CI for Aunty lives at
`.github/workflows/aunty-ci.yml`.
